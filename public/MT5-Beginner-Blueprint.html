Part 2 — Set your stake (lots)

  1. Open EUR/USD chart on 4H
  2. Press F9
  3. Volume: 0.01 (demo micro lot)
  4. Click Stop Loss field → click chart below entry (long) or above (short)
  5. Take Profit at next support/resistance — at least 2× the SL distance
  6. Check risk $ in order window → must be ≤ 1% of balance
  7. Send only if your app says BUY or SELL

0.01 lot on EUR/USD ≈ $0.10 per pip. On $500 account, 20-pip stop ≈ $2 risk.

  • Timeframe visible (4H or Daily)

Avoid

  • Tiny or blurry crop
  • Multiple timeframes in one image

Part 5 — When to END trades

  • TP hit — done, no FOMO re-entry
  • SL hit — accept loss, no revenge
  • Invalidation — close beyond signal wick
  • Doji at profit — take profit (Bible: indecision)
  • 2:1 R:R reached — move SL to breakeven optional
  • 2 losses same day — stop trading

Part 6 — 4-week beginner path

  • Week 1: MT5 demo, Market Watch, F9, 0.01 lots only
  • Week 2: 4H only, mark S/R, screenshot → app before every demo trade
  • Week 3: Add Daily top-down; trade only when app + eyes agree
  • Week 4: Journal 20+ trades; no live money until 3 profitable demo months

Part 7 — Daily workflow

DEMO → Daily structure → 4H levels → App screenshot → Checklist pass? → Tools tab (session + R:R) → F9 order → History tab journal.

Golden rule: App says STAY OUT → you do not trade. No exceptions while learning.
